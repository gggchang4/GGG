# Audio sources

The MP3 files in this directory are public-domain / CC0 preview tracks from
[FreePD](https://en.freepd.cn/music). They are used as clearly labelled audio
studies for the interactive record-player demo; they are not recordings from
the commercial albums shown by the sleeve artwork.

Downloaded on 2026-08-11 from the FreePD electronic collection:

- `3 am West End`
- `Arpent`
- `Backbeat`
- `Beat One`
- `Bit Bit Loop`
- `Chronos`
- `Favorite`
- `Fireworks`
- `Goodnightmare`
- `Hear What They Say`
- `Hippety Hop`

FreePD states that these tracks are released under CC0, may be used in apps and
commercial projects, and do not require attribution. This file keeps the
provenance visible even though attribution is optional.
